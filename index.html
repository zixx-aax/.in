📖 Documentation & User Guide

How do I create a permanent cloud link? ▼
Click 'Generate Link', choose Permanent, enter your password (L), optionally give your link a custom name (and a visitor passcode if you have Premium), and the platform will generate a permanent cloud URL carrying your branded watermark footer.
How do I fast-update or reuse older deployment versions? ▼
Tap any item inside your 🚀 Deployed Codes sidebar to view every saved version (v1, v2, v3...), inspect or reuse any old version in the editor, or tap ✏️ Open & Fast-Edit to edit/paste your new code and push it live without ever typing a Bin ID.
What is the difference between Free and Premium plans? ▼
Free users can deploy up to 2 projects per day with ad support and standard routing. Premium users (1 Day, 1 Week, 1 Month, or 1 Year via one-time redemption codes) get an ad-free studio, unlimited deployments, boosted fast-ping priority routing, Password-Protected Viewer Links, Code Diff comparison, and Pro IDE themes.
Are my local files and deployed codes safe? ▼
All local projects, your active draft, and every deployed code (along with all its v1/v2/v3 version history) are automatically saved inside your browser's local storage. You can also export a JSON backup (including your creator key) anytime from the sidebar to transfer everything safely to another browser or device.
