📖 Documentation & User Guide

How do I create a permanent cloud link? ▼
Type 'permanent' or 'p' when generating a link, enter your password (L), and the platform will generate a permanent cloud URL carrying your branded watermark footer.
Can I edit my code after generating a link? ▼
Yes! Click the 'Edit Link' button in the studio, enter your link or Bin ID along with your password (L), and your code will instantly update without changing the URL.
Are my local files safe? ▼
All local projects are securely saved inside your browser's local storage history so you can retrieve and load them anytime.
